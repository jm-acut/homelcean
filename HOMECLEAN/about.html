About HomeClean

HomeClean is a proposed web-based home cleaning booking and management system created for St. Peter's College SIP 101.

The academic prototype demonstrates public service browsing, customer authentication, password recovery, persistent booking records, and owner-based database access.

Technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Supabase Auth, and PostgreSQL.

This project is not a real cleaning company and does not accept real appointments or payments.
